# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Trade Marketplace is a peer-to-peer marketplace platform similar to kupujemprodajem.com where members can list products for sale and buyers can browse, add items to cart, and contact sellers directly. The platform operates on a token-based economy where 1 token equals 1 RSD (Serbian dinar). There is no direct payment/checkout functionality in v1 - transactions are completed offline after buyer-seller communication.

Key features include:
- Product listings with images and categories
- Shopping cart grouped by seller with direct messaging
- Mutual rating system (buyer rates first, then seller)
- Seller subscription notifications (email/SMS/messaging apps)
- Token balance system for listing fees and promotions
- Tiered pricing (first 15 listings free, then 10 tokens each)

## Glossary

- **Member**: A registered user who can act as both Buyer and Seller
- **Buyer**: A Member browsing and adding products to cart
- **Seller**: A Member who lists products for sale
- **Product**: An item listed for sale with name, category, price, description, and images
- **Cart**: A temporary collection of products grouped by Seller
- **Token**: Virtual currency unit (1 Token = 1 RSD/dinar)
- **Balance**: The Token amount in a Member's account
- **Rating**: A 5-star evaluation with mandatory comment
- **Transaction**: A completed exchange initiated via messaging
- **Subscription**: A Buyer following a Seller to receive notifications
- **Promotion**: A paid feature to highlight a Product listing
- **Activation**: The action of making a Product visible to Buyers
- **Free_Tier_Limit**: The first 15 Products a Seller can list without Token cost
- **Listing_Fee**: 10 Tokens charged per Product beyond Free_Tier_Limit
- **Admin**: A system administrator who configures platform settings

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Member Registration and Authentication

**User Story:** As a visitor, I want to register and log in, so that I can participate as a Buyer or Seller on the platform.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Registration_System SHALL allow visitors to create a Member account with email and password
2. THE Authentication_System SHALL verify Member credentials during login
3. WHEN a Member logs in successfully, THEN THE Authentication_System SHALL create a session for the Member
4. IF invalid credentials are provided, THEN THE Authentication_System SHALL display an error message without revealing which field is incorrect
5. THE Registration_System SHALL validate email format and password strength before creating an account

---

### Requirement 2: Token Balance Management

**User Story:** As a Member, I want to manage my token balance, so that I can pay for listing fees and promotions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Balance_System SHALL maintain a Token balance for each Member where 1 Token equals 1 RSD
2. WHEN a Member views their account, THEN THE Balance_System SHALL display the current Token balance
3. THE Balance_System SHALL allow Members to add Tokens to their balance through a top-up mechanism
4. WHEN a fee is charged, THEN THE Balance_System SHALL deduct Tokens from the Member's balance
5. IF a Member has insufficient balance for an operation, THEN THE Balance_System SHALL prevent the operation and display an error

---

### Requirement 3: Product Listing Creation

**User Story:** As a Seller, I want to create product listings, so that Buyers can discover and purchase my items.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Listing_System SHALL allow Sellers to create a Product with name, category, price, and description
2. THE Listing_System SHALL allow Sellers to upload between 1 and 5 images per Product
3. WHEN a Product is created, THEN THE Listing_System SHALL generate a unique shareable URL for the Product
4. THE Listing_System SHALL validate that all required fields are provided before creating a Product
5. THE Listing_System SHALL store Product images in a cloud storage service

---

### Requirement 4: Listing Limits and Pricing

**User Story:** As a Seller, I want to understand the listing costs, so that I can manage my selling expenses.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Pricing_System SHALL allow Sellers to create up to 15 Products without any Token cost (Free_Tier_Limit)
2. WHEN a Seller creates a Product beyond the Free_Tier_Limit, THEN THE Pricing_System SHALL charge 10 Tokens (Listing_Fee)
3. IF a Seller has insufficient balance for the Listing_Fee, THEN THE Pricing_System SHALL prevent Product creation
4. THE Pricing_System SHALL track the total number of Products created by each Seller
5. THE Pricing_System SHALL display the current listing count and remaining free listings to the Seller

---

### Requirement 5: Product Activation and Deactivation

**User Story:** As a Seller, I want to control the visibility of my products, so that I can manage what Buyers can see.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Activation_System SHALL allow Sellers to activate or deactivate their Products
2. WHEN a Product is deactivated, THEN THE Activation_System SHALL hide it from Buyer searches and browsing
3. THE Activation_System SHALL operate on a monthly billing cycle for active Products
4. WHEN a new billing cycle begins, THEN THE Activation_System SHALL charge applicable fees for active Products beyond Free_Tier_Limit
5. THE Activation_System SHALL notify Sellers before the billing cycle renewal

---

### Requirement 6: Shopping Cart Management

**User Story:** As a Buyer, I want to add products to my cart, so that I can organize items I'm interested in purchasing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Cart_System SHALL allow Buyers to add Products to their cart using "dodaj u korpu" button
2. THE Cart_System SHALL group cart items by Seller
3. WHEN viewing the cart, THEN THE Cart_System SHALL display a "Pošalji poruku" button for each Seller group
4. THE Cart_System SHALL allow Buyers to remove individual items from the cart
5. THE Cart_System SHALL persist cart contents across browser sessions for logged-in Members
6. THE Cart_System SHALL display the total price per Seller group

---

### Requirement 7: Buyer-Seller Messaging

**User Story:** As a Buyer, I want to message Sellers about products, so that I can negotiate and arrange transactions.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Messaging_System SHALL allow Buyers to send messages to Sellers from the cart view
2. WHEN a message is sent from cart, THEN THE Messaging_System SHALL include links and images of the selected Products
3. THE Messaging_System SHALL maintain a conversation thread between Buyer and Seller
4. THE Messaging_System SHALL notify the recipient of new messages
5. THE Messaging_System SHALL allow both parties to view message history

---

### Requirement 8: Rating System

**User Story:** As a Member, I want to rate my transaction partners, so that the community can identify trustworthy members.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Rating_System SHALL use a 5-star scale with mandatory comment for all ratings
2. WHEN a transaction is completed, THEN THE Rating_System SHALL allow the Buyer to rate the Seller first
3. AFTER the Buyer submits a rating, THEN THE Rating_System SHALL allow the Seller to rate the Buyer
4. THE Rating_System SHALL prevent Sellers from rating Buyers before receiving a Buyer rating
5. THE Rating_System SHALL display average rating and total rating count on Member profiles
6. THE Rating_System SHALL display individual ratings with comments on Member profiles

---

### Requirement 9: Seller Subscription and Notifications

**User Story:** As a Buyer, I want to subscribe to Sellers, so that I can receive notifications about their new products.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Subscription_System SHALL allow Buyers to subscribe to Sellers
2. WHEN a subscribed Seller lists a new Product, THEN THE Notification_System SHALL notify subscribed Buyers
3. THE Notification_System SHALL support email, SMS, Viber, and WhatsApp notification channels
4. THE Notification_System SHALL have all notification channels disabled by default
5. THE Notification_System SHALL allow Members to enable/disable individual notification channels
6. THE Subscription_System SHALL allow Buyers to unsubscribe from Sellers

---

### Requirement 10: Product Promotion

**User Story:** As a Seller, I want to promote my products, so that they receive more visibility.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Promotion_System SHALL allow Sellers to promote Products for 300 Tokens per Product
2. THE Promotion_System SHALL limit each Seller to a maximum of 3 promoted Products at a time
3. WHEN a Product is promoted, THEN THE Promotion_System SHALL display it in a highlighted section
4. IF a Seller attempts to promote more than 3 Products, THEN THE Promotion_System SHALL prevent the action
5. THE Promotion_System SHALL allow Sellers to remove promotion from a Product

---

### Requirement 11: Product Browsing and Search

**User Story:** As a Buyer, I want to browse and search products, so that I can find items I want to purchase.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Search_System SHALL allow Buyers to search Products by keyword
2. THE Search_System SHALL allow Buyers to filter Products by category
3. THE Search_System SHALL allow Buyers to sort Products by price, date, and relevance
4. THE Search_System SHALL only display active Products in search results
5. THE Search_System SHALL display promoted Products in a highlighted section above regular results
6. THE Search_System SHALL paginate results for performance

---

### Requirement 12: Seller Profile and Product Management

**User Story:** As a Seller, I want to manage my profile and products, so that I can maintain my marketplace presence.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Profile_System SHALL display Seller information including name, rating, and member since date
2. THE Profile_System SHALL display all active Products listed by the Seller
3. THE Management_System SHALL allow Sellers to edit their Product details
4. THE Management_System SHALL allow Sellers to delete their Products
5. THE Profile_System SHALL display the Seller's subscription count

---

### Requirement 13: Data Persistence

**User Story:** As a system, I want to persist data appropriately, so that information is stored reliably and efficiently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Persistence_System SHALL store all data (structured and document data) in a relational database (PostgreSQL)
2. THE Persistence_System SHALL use free-tier database services
3. THE Persistence_System SHALL implement appropriate indexes for query performance
4. THE Persistence_System SHALL backup data regularly

---

### Requirement 14: Technology Stack

**User Story:** As a developer, I want a defined technology stack, so that the application is built consistently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Application SHALL be built using Node.js for the backend runtime
2. THE Application SHALL use Next.js as the full-stack framework
3. THE Application SHALL use React.js for the frontend UI components
4. THE Application SHALL use free-tier cloud services for hosting and storage
5. THE Application SHALL use free-tier notification services for email/SMS/messaging
6. THE Application SHALL generate unique shareable URLs for each Product listing

---

### Requirement 15: Admin Configuration

**User Story:** As an Admin, I want to configure platform settings, so that I can manage the marketplace operations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to configure the Free_Tier_Limit value
2. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to configure the Listing_Fee value
3. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to configure the Promotion cost
4. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to manage Product categories
5. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to view platform statistics and reports
6. THE Admin_System SHALL allow Admins to manage Member accounts (suspend/activate)
